# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a welcoming, technically rigorous community. Participate respectfully, assume good faith, give specific feedback, and make room for different experience levels and viewpoints.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, publishing private information, and deliberately unsafe guidance—especially guidance that could cause unauthorized trading or expose credentials.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation to protect the community. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's configured security or contact channel.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant. Community participation is also subject to applicable law and platform rules.
